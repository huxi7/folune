# Color Lab v0

> Captured 2026-08-14 CST from the isolated local `/color-lab` route. No authentication, production request or external write is involved.

## Comparison contract

- Ink Blue and Archivist Green render the same static Library, Paper and AI structure.
- Copy, typography, spacing, radius, Paper shadow and durable neutral backgrounds are identical.
- Light and Dark only change scoped Primary, Soft, focus and non-danger Loading tokens.
- The route exists only under `import.meta.env.DEV`; `/`, `/app`, authentication and business state remain untouched.

## Evidence

- `color-lab-light.png`: Split Light comparison at 1280×880.
- `color-lab-dark.png`: Split Dark comparison at 1280×880.

These images are a v0 decision aid, not final acceptance evidence. Production colors must not change until one direction is explicitly selected.
